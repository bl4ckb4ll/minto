# Barbara Minto — _The Pyramid Principle_

Also referred to here informally as the **Minto method**.

## Why it is here

The useful practice is simple enough to state without mystique:

1. lead with the answer or controlling thought;
2. group supporting ideas into a small number of coherent reasons;
3. order those reasons deliberately;
4. push detail downward instead of making every sentence compete for equal importance.

The point of keeping the book here is to study the practice critically, not to treat its author or terminology as canonical.
