# George Orwell — _Homage to Catalonia_

## Why it is here

If Orwell is going to matter to this repository, use the Orwell who tried to report what he had actually seen and to distinguish observation, inference, allegiance, uncertainty, and retrospective correction.

_Homage to Catalonia_ is therefore a better companion for this project than treating Orwell mainly through _Animal Farm_ or through the adjective “Orwellian.”

The book is useful here for questions such as:

- How do you write forcefully while admitting what you do not know?
- How do you describe political conflict without pretending to stand outside it?
- How do you distinguish direct observation from second-hand claims?
- How do you revise your account when later evidence changes what you think happened?
- How much background does a reader actually need before the argument disappears under exposition?

## Repository status

A full-text copy is **not committed here** unless we have a copy whose redistribution is permitted. If a lawful distributable copy becomes available, store it in this directory and record its provenance here.
