# Allen Hatcher — *Algebraic Topology*

## Source anchor
Hatcher makes the book and individual chapters available from Cornell. Chapter 0 is explicitly "Geometric Notions"; Chapters 1–4 then develop fundamental group, homology, cohomology, and homotopy theory.

## What to study
- geometric setup before machinery;
- diagrams and examples as part of the argument;
- informal prose used to keep the reader oriented;
- chapter dependency design.

## First structural experiment
Take a single later theorem and construct two paths to it:

1. **native path** — preserve Hatcher's surrounding development;
2. **goal path** — retain only definitions, examples, lemmas, and diagrams needed for that theorem.

Compare:
- number of forward references;
- number of definitions encountered before their purpose is visible;
- amount of mathematical context lost by the goal path;
- whether the shorter path is actually easier to remember.

This is a good test case for "sub-book" construction because the original is already unusually navigable. A remix has to beat a strong baseline rather than merely repair bad prose.
