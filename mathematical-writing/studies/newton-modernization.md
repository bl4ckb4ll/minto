# Newton — translation, reconstruction, and mathematical modernization

Two distinct references may be relevant here.

## Cohen–Whitman
I. Bernard Cohen and Anne Whitman's 1999 *Principia* is a modern English translation of Newton's third edition, with a substantial guide by Cohen.

Study question:
- how far can language and notation be modernized before the reader loses contact with Newton's actual mathematical method?

## Chandrasekhar
S. Chandrasekhar's *Newton's Principia for the Common Reader* (1995) is by a major physicist, but it is an analysis/reconstruction rather than the modern translation itself.

This may be the physicist remembered in the initial discussion.

Study question:
- when is reconstruction better exposition than translation?
- what mathematical content becomes clearer when a modern expert rebuilds an old argument in contemporary terms?
- what historical information disappears?
