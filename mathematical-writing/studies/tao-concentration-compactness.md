# Terence Tao — concentration compactness

## Source anchor
Tao's 2008 expository post "Concentration compactness and the profile decomposition" begins from sequential compactness, immediately states a concrete extremiser proposition, and then develops the failure of compactness that motivates concentration compactness.

## Why this belongs in the mixed set
There is a strong piece of exposition here: the abstract concept is attached quickly to a mathematical use. The reader sees why compactness matters before the more technical machinery arrives.

The repair question is different from the mathematical decomposition.

## Experiment

### A. Source-structure paraphrase

### B. Architecture-first

- maximizing sequences escape ordinary compactness;
- identify the failure modes;
- then introduce concentration compactness as the classification/repair mechanism.

### C. Example-first
Begin with one concrete sequence exhibiting loss of compactness, then generalize.

Evaluate whether B or C reduces the feeling of procedural accumulation while preserving Tao's useful decomposition of the technical problem.
